# Weather Z – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Weather Z is a free weather app for Android. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no
tracking. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

## What the app sends, and to whom

The app talks to one service only: **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com), which provides the weather
data.

- **Your location.** When you use "My location", the app reads your device's approximate
  location (it never asks for precise location) and sends those coordinates to the
  Open-Meteo forecast API to get the weather for that place. Location is used only while the
  app is open; the app never reads it in the background. You can deny the permission and
  use city search instead.
- **City searches.** When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to the Open-Meteo
  geocoding API to find matching places.
- **Saved cities.** To get their forecasts, the app sends the coordinates of your saved
  cities to the Open-Meteo forecast API.

Like any internet request, these reach Open-Meteo together with your IP address. Open-Meteo's
own privacy policy applies to what they receive: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms

The developer of Weather Z has no server and does not receive, collect or store any of
your data.

## What stays on your phone

The app stores the following on your device only:

- your list of saved cities and which one you looked at last;
- the last forecast for each place (including the coordinates of your last location fix),
  so the app can show weather when you are offline.

None of this leaves your phone except as described above. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its
data in Android settings, deletes all of it.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
- No accounts or sign-in.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
- No background location access.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.
